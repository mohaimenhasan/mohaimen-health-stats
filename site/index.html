    Methods

    • Weight: self-reported monthly log, plus scale entries in Apple Health and DEXA. Daily values are interpolated.
    • Workouts: Apple Health and Strava are merged; overlapping sessions recorded by multiple apps are de-duplicated (Apple Watch preferred). Sessions without energy data (e.g. Strava-only recordings) get an estimate from heart rate, fitted on my own sessions of the same type (shown with ~).
    • Local times use the time zone of the nearest Apple Watch workout, since the Health export stores everything in the exporting phone's offset.
    • Max HR is estimated from the 99.9th percentile of in-workout heart rate. Zones are 60/70/80/90% of max HR and are only computed when HR sampling is dense.
    • Football speed metrics come from Strava GPX tracks only (Apple Watch soccer routes lack per-point timing). Speeds use displacement over a 2-second window with plausibility filters; GPS still overestimates short bursts.
    • Correlations are monthly and observational — they don't imply causation.

    Privacy

    • Only aggregate numbers are published. No GPS coordinates, locations, contacts, clinical records or birth date leave the build machine.
